Aurivox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026
The short version: Aurivox collects nothing, because there is nowhere for anything to go. Aurivox has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your recordings and transcripts never leave your computer.

What Aurivox does with your audio

Aurivox transcribes audio and video files entirely on your own device, using AI models that you download to your computer. Your audio files, your transcripts, and your library are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared by the app.

Information we collect

None. Aurivox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, audio, transcripts, or device identifiers. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting services.

Downloads and subscription checks

The only network activity in Aurivox is:

  1. Downloading AI model files (from Hugging Face, a public model host) when you choose to download a model in Settings or during first-run setup. No personal information is sent with these downloads beyond what any file download involves (such as your IP address, handled by the host under its own privacy policy).
  2. Checking your subscription status with Microsoft, and opening Microsoft's checkout if you choose to subscribe. Subscriptions are sold and managed entirely by Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy policy; Aurivox never receives or stores your payment information. The app only learns whether a Pro subscription is active.
  3. Checking whether a newer version of a model file is available.

Your data on your device

Your transcript library, settings, and free-credit counter are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Uninstalling Aurivox removes the app; downloaded model files can be deleted from within Settings at any time before uninstalling.

Children

Aurivox does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Aurivox ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the changes will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the support email shown on the Aurivox listing in the Microsoft Store.

Aurivox runs 100% on your device. There is no Aurivox server.
